# Exercise 05 — Credential Exposure

**Objective:** teach why plaintext authentication protocols are dangerous.

Use only the dedicated dummy accounts created for this lab. Observe traffic only on the isolated host-only network and only during the instructor's demonstration window.

Students should explain:
- why plaintext credentials can be observed
- how HTTPS and SFTP change the risk
- credential rotation after exposure
- detection opportunities for insecure protocols

Never capture, store or reuse real credentials.
