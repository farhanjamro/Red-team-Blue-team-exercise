# Exercise 01 — Port Scanning

**Objective:** identify the victim's exposed attack surface.

Red team: conduct an authorized Nmap scan of the private victim IP and record discovered services.

Blue team: correlate the scan with packet captures and service logs where available.

Expected learning:
- ports map to services
- service/version information helps defenders prioritize exposure
- scanning can be detected by repeated connection attempts

Deliverable: service inventory plus hardening recommendations.
